## Signals
The submissions reveal a maturing ecosystem where **agent infrastructure** (harnesses, memory, context runtimes) is becoming as important as model capability. **Training-free self-improvement** is emerging as a practical paradigm: verifier-harness co-evolution, contrastive decoding, and recursive specification refinement all achieve meaningful gains without weight updates. **Safety is shifting from output filtering to internal monitoring** — residual-stream probes, provenance-governed memory, and entity-binding audits catch violations before they propagate. **Multi-agent systems are adopting reusable, composable abstractions** (options, carriers, specialized roles) rather than monolithic loops, with explicit handling of unreliable collaborators. **Benchmarks are moving beyond pass/fail** to measure process fidelity (clinical workflow, rule reasoning, behavioral equivalence, say-do gaps), exposing failures invisible to terminal metrics. Finally, **inference efficiency** is being attacked at multiple layers: diffusion LLM decoding, mobile parallel decoding, verified retry strategies, and progress-aware RL shaping all trade compute for capability under realistic constraints.
