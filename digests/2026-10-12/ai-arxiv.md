## Research Trend Signal

Three convergent directions define the emerging frontier. First, **agent self-improvement is moving from weight updates to structured belief and harness evolution**: EvoBelief's falsifiable hypotheses, Evo²'s weight-harness co-evolution, and RTI's rollout-time interventions all bypass the on-policy exploration bottleneck by leveraging execution feedback, environmental validation, or reward-side evidence to generate learning signals beyond the policy's current reach. Second, **role specialization and explicit state management are replacing monolithic tool-use**: KnowForge's Locator/Judge/Reader pipeline, LongHarness's Evidence-Conclusion Coevolution, and ChemDiffAgent's diffusion-based joint reasoning/planning demonstrate that separating concerns—retrieval vs. reading, evidence vs. conclusion, planning vs. execution—yields disproportionate gains on long-horizon, multimodal, and scientific tasks. Third, **evaluation is becoming generative, adversarial, and grounded in real-world consequences**: SWE-Duel's self-scaling arena, DRYLAB-Bench's wet-lab-validated biosecurity scoring, SpireBench's commercial game harness, and the demonstration of cross-agent reward-hacking transmission all treat benchmarks as living systems where difficulty, risk, and strategic depth emerge from agent interactions rather than fixed test sets. Together, these trends point toward agents that continuously restructure their own cognitive architecture, maintain auditable world models, and are evaluated in environments that co-evolve with their capabilities.
