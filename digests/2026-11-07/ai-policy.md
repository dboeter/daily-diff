## Highlights

The Dutch Cabinet published its AI vision (*Kabinetsvisie op Artificial Intelligence – kiezen voor AI voor iedereen*) alongside formal responses to the SER advice on work and AI and to the AI Deltaplan and Europe2031 report, signalling a national push for “open strategic autonomy” while acknowledging persistent US and Chinese influence. The European Commission preliminarily found TikTok’s recommender system in breach of the Digital Services Act for creating harmful “rabbit holes,” marking the first time the Commission has targeted a platform’s algorithmic recommendations as a DSA violation. Two EU consultations close soon: the Articles 75c–75d AI Act Implementing Act (9 November) and the Cloud and AI Development Act (11 November).
