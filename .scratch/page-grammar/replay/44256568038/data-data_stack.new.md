## Soda Core
- **Release**: [v4.31.0](https://github.com/sodadata/soda-core/releases/tag/v4.31.0) fixes freshness checks to report `NOT_EVALUATED` when the max timestamp was not measured, and now exits with code 3 when Soda Cloud rejects results for a customer-caused reason.

## OpenMetadata
- **News**: [Announcing OpenMetadata 2.1: Your Business Definitions in the Context Graph](https://blog.open-metadata.org/announcing-openmetadata-2-1-your-business-definitions-in-the-context-graph-54c65a6c6749) — OpenMetadata frames 2.1 around putting business definitions in the context graph, arguing that without that business context an AI agent asked for last quarter's revenue may find several revenue columns and can return a plausible but wrong figure.
