## Highlights
- OpenAI's GPT-6.2 series is now on OpenRouter with a 1.1M-token context, spanning the flagship Astra, the everyday model Sol (an upgrade on GPT-6.1 Sol), and Pro variants that run a higher reasoning mode for complex tasks.
