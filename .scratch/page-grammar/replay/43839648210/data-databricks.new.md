## Terraform provider for Databricks
- **Release:** [v1.143.0](https://github.com/databricks/terraform-provider-databricks/releases/tag/v1.143.0): `databricks_app` now updates through the asynchronous Apps update API, so `compute_size`, `compute_min_instances`, and `compute_max_instances` are applied and omitted updatable fields are cleared, including `compute_size` resetting to `MEDIUM`, `compute_min_instances` and `compute_max_instances` disabling horizontal scaling, and `git_source` being cleared; fixes `databricks_connection` to keep configured `pem_key_passphrase`, `ssl_keystore_key`, `ssl_key_password`, and `schema_registry_api_secret`; fixes `databricks_connection` to ignore whitespace-only differences in PEM-encoded options such as `userProvidedServerCertificate`.
