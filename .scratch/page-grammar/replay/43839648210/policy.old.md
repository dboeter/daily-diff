## Highlights

The European Commission opened a consultation on draft revised election guidelines under the Digital Services Act ([Commission invites views on draft revised election guidelines under Digital Services Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-invites-views-draft-revised-election-guidelines-under-digital-services-act)), aiming to address the rapid evolution of online electoral processes. The initiative targets very large online platforms and search engines, seeking stakeholder input on updated mitigation measures for systemic risks related to electoral integrity.
