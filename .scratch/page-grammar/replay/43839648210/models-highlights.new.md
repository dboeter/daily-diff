## Highlights

- Qwen4 Max, part of Alibaba's Qwen4 series, is newly listed on OpenRouter with a 1M-token context and text, image, and video input; Qwen says it also adds image generation, document processing, web search, tool use, and artifacts.
- A Baseten article maps how batch sizing, parallelism, quantization, kernels, speculative decoding, and disaggregation each shift the latency-throughput frontier of LLM inference.
- A Sebastian Raschka article walks through the positional-embedding methods modern LLMs use to represent token positions.
