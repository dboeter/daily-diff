## Highlights
- An issue reports that premium seat usage limits (Team/Enterprise) are being exhausted roughly 3–5x faster on non-Fable models (Opus/Sonnet) since the week of November 9, 2026.
- − Oh My Pi v18.21.0–v18.22.0 removes the legacy standalone `ida` and `ghidra` disassembler tools, the old `PlanReviewOverlay` options and the Tern plan review sheet's Copy and Edit-in-editor buttons.
- OpenCode v1.19.6 offers an in-app upgrade to OpenCode 2.0 that downloads in the background and installs only when you choose to upgrade, keeping sessions, projects and settings, though the release notes say plugins made for OpenCode 1 need an update.
