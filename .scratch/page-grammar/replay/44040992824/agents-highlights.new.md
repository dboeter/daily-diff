## Highlights

- An issue reports that Claude Code premium seat usage limits are being exhausted roughly 3–5x faster on non-Fable models since the week of November 9, 2026, which the reporter attributes to a metering change rather than changed workloads.
- Oh My Pi v18.21–v18.22 moves binary analysis to a backend-neutral dsm namespace and renames its ida.* settings to disassembler.*, a breaking change.
