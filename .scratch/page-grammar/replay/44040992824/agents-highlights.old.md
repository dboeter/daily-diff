## Highlights
- Oh My Pi v18.21.0–v18.22.0 introduces breaking changes: binary analysis moves to the `dsm` namespace, `ida.*` settings are replaced by `disassembler.*`, legacy standalone disassembler tools and `PlanReviewOverlay` options are removed, and the `AgentSession` run lifecycle is reworked with new events and methods (`agent_end`, `agent_pause`, `isRunActive`, `wakeSources`, `waitForRunEnd()`).
- Claude Code hot issue #110480 reports premium seat usage limits (Team/Enterprise) are being exhausted roughly 3–5× faster on non-Fable models (Opus/Sonnet) since the week of November 9, 2026, which the reporter attributes to a metering change rather than workload shifts.
- jcode v1.0.0 launches jcode Cloud (`/cloud` moves a session or swarm to a VM, `/local` brings it back), reworks swarms to plan in phases and parallel lanes with a pinned team progress view, and claims 25% less CPU, idle wakeups down from 2.68 to 1.51/sec, and rendered-span memory in a large conversation down from 20.4 MB to 14.6 MB.
