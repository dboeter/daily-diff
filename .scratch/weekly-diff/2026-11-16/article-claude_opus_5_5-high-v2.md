## Claude Fable 5.2 and the coding agents

Claude Code [v2.1.321–v2.1.322](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.321) make Claude Fable 5.2 the default model, with 1M context at $8/$40 per Mtok. OpenRouter listed [Claude Fable 5.2](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-fable-5.2) on 2026-11-09 and describes it as Anthropic's model with gains over Fable 5.1 in coding, reasoning, and vision. Anthropic's announcement says Fable 5.2 achieved a new benchmark in coding, finance, and science. An analysis [reports a score of 65 on Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2), the highest to date.

The rollout drew issue reports. In [#108726](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/108726), users report that `DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1` stops feature flags from loading. As a result, the `/model` picker hides Fable 5.2 and falls back to compiled defaults. [Issue #109446](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/109446) says version 2.1.323 silently removed `subagentStatusLine` with no replacement, which hides per-subagent context, cost, model, effort, and status. The status page [confirmed elevated errors](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50481187) across Claude services.

Usage figures moved unevenly. In OpenRouter's app ranking, [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code) sat at week #4 with 10.80T tokens/week (+7.5% vs previous week). [Hermes Agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) sat at week #3 with 13.02T tokens/week (+5.1% vs previous week). [Kilo Code](https://kilocode.ai/) reached 7.03T tokens/week (+46% vs previous week), the largest rise in that table, and [Strix](https://strix.ai/) reached 2.56T tokens/week (+45% vs previous week). [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai/) fell to 2.69T tokens/week (-46% vs previous week). [omp](https://omp.sh/) fell to 2.13T tokens/week (-36% vs previous week) on OpenRouter, while its npm downloads rose to 295,519 downloads/week (+34% vs previous week).

_The telemetry report matters for teams that disable telemetry, because it touches which model they see as the new default. The removed status line suggests that tooling built on Claude Code's status output can break between releases. omp's opposite movements on OpenRouter and npm in one week suggest the two sources capture different kinds of use._

## New models in BigQuery, Snowflake and Databricks

BigQuery generative AI functions [now support `gemini-4-carbon`](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/release-notes#November_10_2026) for generally available use. They also [support `gemini-4-hydrogen`](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/release-notes#November_12_2026), which is generally available. OpenRouter describes [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://openrouter.ai/google/gemini-4-hydrogen) as a high-efficiency workhorse model of the Gemini 4 family, built for high-throughput agentic workflows, coding, and multi-model routing at lower cost and latency. Snowflake made [Anthropic Claude 5.5 and OpenAI GPT-6](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-11-06-claude-5-5-openai-gpt-6-ga) generally available.

On Azure Databricks, [Zhipu AI GLM 5.2](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/databricks/release-notes/product/2026/november#zhipu-ai-glm-52-is-scheduled-for-retirement) is deprecated and will be retired on 2027-02-04, with migration to GLM 5.3. Databricks says [Smart Routing](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/databricks/release-notes/unity-gateway#smart-routing-is-faster-and-lower-cost-and-now-routes-subagents-only-beta) now routes only subagents instead of the first prompt. According to Databricks, this reduces cost by up to 70% and cuts p90 latency from 9 seconds to 2 seconds. Smart Routing for Codex is paused.

_BigQuery added both Gemini 4 models on the same dates OpenRouter listed them, which suggests new Google models reach SQL functions with little delay. The Gemini 3.6 Flash redirect cuts the other way: calls keep running, but they reach a different model on 2026-11-19, so a pipeline can change model without a code change._

## Azure Databricks deadlines

After 2026-11-30, requests to the Genie One MCP Beta endpoint at `/api/2.0/mcp/genie` fail. Databricks points workloads to the `system.ai.genie_one_mcp` MCP in Unity Gateway. Starting on or after 2026-12-10, [external Delta clients](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/databricks/release-notes/whats-coming#external-access-to-unity-catalog-managed-delta-tables-will-soon-be-available-by-default-for-workspaces-with-the-compliance-security-profile-enabled) can create, read and write Unity Catalog managed Delta tables by default in workspaces with the compliance security profile enabled. Creating and writing requires catalog commits.

After Databricks Runtime 15.4 LTS reaches end of support on 2027-08-19, [all Unity Catalog managed tables will be automatically upgraded to catalog commits](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/databricks/release-notes/whats-coming#all-unity-catalog-managed-tables-will-be-upgraded-to-catalog-commits). The release note calls this a breaking change. Separately, Databricks CLI [v1.25.0](https://github.com/databricks/cli/releases/tag/v1.25.0) removes `CRITICAL` as a valid `compute.priority_class` value.

_Catalog commits appear twice here: as the condition for external writes from December, and as the automatic upgrade in 2027. This suggests Databricks is making catalog commits the basis for how other engines write to managed tables._

## Security reports in the data and agent stack

A [write-up](https://mehmetince.net/part-3-6-how-i-ended-up-turning-a-22-year-old-postgresql-type-confusion-primitive-into-rce-cve-2026-6460/) describes a 22-year-old zero-day type-confusion vulnerability in PostgreSQL core that enables remote code execution. Separately, the PostgreSQL Global Development Group [released 18.7, 17.12, 16.16, 15.20, 14.25 and 19 RC 2](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/postgresql-187-1712-1616-1520-1425-and-19-rc-2-released-3442/). Claude Code [v2.1.325](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.325) fixes a security issue in zstd-compressed response decoding.

In Hermes Agent, [an issue](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/issues/62336) says terminal environment snapshots write all environment variables to persistent plaintext files. That includes credential-bearing variables from Bitwarden Secrets Manager. [Another issue](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/issues/152674) says the Desktop app admits chat messages from two different accounts as `uid:10000`.

_The Hermes items are reports, not confirmed flaws, but both concern credentials or identity on the user's own machine. The write-up places the PostgreSQL flaw in core, so its reach would not depend on a single extension._

## EU and Dutch AI rules

The Commission adopted [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2487](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026R2487), which sets rules for AI regulatory sandboxes under the AI Act. It also published [preparatory guidelines](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52026XC06005) on the transparency obligations of Article 50. On 2026-12-02, the AI Act [bans on sexual deepfakes and CSAM generators](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/timeline/timeline-implementation-eu-ai-act) apply, and the Art. 50(2) transition ends.

In the Netherlands, the [cabinet adopted a vision for AI](https://www.digitaleoverheid.nl/nieuws/kabinet-kiest-voor-ai-die-nederland-vooruithelpt/). The Tweede Kamer received a [motion by El Boujdaini](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/detail?id=2026Z24543&did=2026D56682) calling for authorities to be sufficiently equipped to supervise the AI Regulation. It also received a [motion by Dassen](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/detail?id=2026Z24563&did=2026D56703) calling for a legal framework for liability for damage caused by AI models.

_The Article 50 guidelines arrive less than three weeks before the Art. 50(2) transition ends, which puts the guidance and the deadline close together for clients whose systems fall under that article. The Dutch motions show parliamentary attention to supervision capacity and liability, but they are motions, not rules._
