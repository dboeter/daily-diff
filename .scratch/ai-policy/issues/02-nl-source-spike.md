# Netherlands source verification spike

Status: resolved
Type: research

Same questions as `01-source-spike.md`, for Dutch AI-policy sources. Read `../spec.md` (Decisions, reader profile) first: EU and NL weigh more than the US, so NL coverage should be as good as the EU's.

For each candidate source:

- Access: endpoint/feed URL, auth (key? cost), rate limits, terms of use for automated access.
- Shape: a stable item id, a stage/status, a date of the latest action, a title, a link, and for consultations a deadline.
- Diffability: an updated-since filter, an RSS/Atom feed, or snapshot and compare.
- Filtering: how well a Dutch keyword query (e.g. "kunstmatige intelligentie", "algoritme", "AI-verordening") separates AI-policy items from noise, measured on one real recent week (and 90 days where a week is too small).
- Language: everything will be Dutch; note any English versions.

Candidates (none verified):

- Tweede Kamer open data (OData API / SyncFeed): bills (wetsvoorstellen), motions, Kamerbrieven, committee debates. Which object gives a bill's stage (plenary vote, passed the Tweede Kamer)?
- Eerste Kamer: passage of bills.
- officielebekendmakingen.nl / KOOP SRU API (Staatsblad, Staatscourant, Kamerstukken).
- internetconsultatie.nl: consultations with deadlines.
- Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens: news, and the Rapportage AI- & Algoritmerisico's Nederland (the AP coordinates algorithm/AI supervision).
- Rijksoverheid news / ministry of BZK and EZ (Digitale Overheid, the AI Act implementation in NL).
- Rijksinspectie Digitale Infrastructuur (RDI): the market-surveillance role under the AI Act.
- Authoritative Dutch news/commentary on AI policy with RSS (e.g. Tweakers, iBestuur, FD, NRC, Computable, law-firm blogs) — paywall and volume.

Output under `## Answer`: a table per source with a recommendation (use / use with caveat / drop), plus a short overall recommendation and any keys needed. Then set `Status: resolved`.

## Answer

Spike run 2026-10-14. Every source here is keyless and was called directly; nothing needed a signup. "Noise" means keyword hits versus items that are actually about AI policy, judged from titles. The measured week is 2026-10-06..12 unless stated; 90 days is 2026-07-15..10-13. Anything marked _unverified_ was not read or run. All sources are Dutch-only unless noted.

### Overall recommendation

- **Parliament (the core):** Tweede Kamer OData. Keyword discovery on `Titel`/`Onderwerp` for motions, government letters and questions (~2–5 hits a week, mostly on target), then Jev. Bills by **dossier-number watchlist** with the stage from `Besluit.BesluitSoort`, reported from "Stemmen - aangenomen" on: no NL AI bill carries an AI keyword in its title. Watchlist now: 36875 (Uitvoeringswet verordening cyberweerbaarheid, passed the TK 2026-09-10, in the Senate since 2026-09-22); add the Uitvoeringswet AI-verordening as soon as it appears as a `Wetgeving` Zaak.
- **Senate and enactment:** KOOP SRU per watchlisted dossier (`w.dossiernummer`): the Eerste Kamer's `kst-<nr>-A` marks arrival, `stb-` marks enactment. Diff on `dt.available`, never `issued`. Not for discovery (full-text noise, 13,901 publications a week).
- **Consultations and deadlines:** snapshot the internetconsultatie.nl homepage (slug + `Sluitingsdatum`), every new slug to Jev. No API or feed exists.
- **Regulators:** AP RSS (every item to Jev); RDI and Rijksoverheid (BZK, EZ) once their new feed URLs are copied from the site's RSS button — a one-time manual step, because the platform change of 2026-06-02 broke every old feed URL. Digitale Overheid RSS as a low-volume extra.
- **News:** Binnenlands Bestuur; drop Tweakers, NRC (stale whole-site feed, paywalled) and FD (home feed, likely paywalled).
- **Keys:** none. No NL source needs a key or a signup. Rate limits and terms of use were not published on any endpoint tried: _unverified_ everywhere.
